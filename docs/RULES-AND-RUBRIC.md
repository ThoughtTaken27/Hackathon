# Rules and rubric

Checked against the official site on Nov 13, 2026: [About](https://highschoolhackathon.org/about.html), [FAQ](https://highschoolhackathon.org/faq), [Judges](https://highschoolhackathon.org/judges.html). All official times are Eastern.

## Rules

- **All project code must be written during the 48 hours.** Learning, practice and team formation beforehand are fine. Anything built before kickoff (e.g. a model trained earlier) must be rebuilt after kickoff.
- **Teams have 1 to 3 members.** Teammates can be from different schools or countries.
- **AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) are allowed**, "the same way working engineers do". You must understand and be able to explain everything. Finalists are asked about their choices live.
- **Public datasets, open-source libraries and pretrained models are allowed, if credited.**
- **Late submissions are not accepted.** Submit at least an hour early. No exceptions for last-minute technical issues.
- Tracks include health, finance and climate, plus others. The specific challenge per track drops at kickoff. Pick a track by Nov 14.
- Everything is virtual, including the finals.

## What we submit (before 11:59 PM ET Sun Nov 15)

1. **Working code:** a Google Colab notebook or a GitHub repo the judges can open and run.
2. **A one-page write-up:** the problem, our approach, and how well it worked.
3. **A 2-minute demo video:** an unlisted YouTube link or a direct upload.

## Round 1 rubric (blind review, Nov 16–18), 100 points

| Criterion | Points | What it means for us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical execution: does it work, is it well built? | 30 | One working end-to-end flow; clean, runnable repo/Colab |
| Originality: a fresh take? | 20 | Not on Veritas's "common ideas" list; a clear twist |
| Impact and track fit | 20 | A real, specific user; tied to the brief's wording |
| Testing and honesty: did you measure it, do you know where it fails? | 15 | Test set, metrics, baseline, fairness table, failure cases |
| Communication: clear write-up and video? | 15 | Numbers visible early; one page; under 2:00 |

Names and schools are hidden in Round 1. Don't put them in the video or write-up if avoidable.

## Finals rubric (live, Sat Nov 21), 100 points

5-minute live demo, then judges' questions.

| Criterion | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Demo and clarity | 30 |
| Technical depth | 30 |
| Impact | 20 |
| Q&A | 20 |

**Final ranking = 70% finals + 30% Round 1.** Judges include researchers from Microsoft and Microsoft Research with Harvard, Princeton and Stanford backgrounds. Expect sharp technical questions.

## Prizes

Gold team: a remote internship at Arecibo AI (a healthcare AI startup; its app supports older adults with cognitive impairment) for every member. Also scholarships from a $75,000+ pool. Claim by Dec 6. Health + older adults fits Arecibo's focus well.
