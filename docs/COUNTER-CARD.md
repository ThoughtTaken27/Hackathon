# Counter card (print, fold, stand next to the kitchen laptop)

---

## This laptop listens for kitchen sounds.

It notes only the **kind** of sound, like "dishes" or "TV".
It keeps **no recordings and no words**.

Its owner chose to turn it on, and can pause it or turn it off any time,
using the big **Pause** button on the screen.

*Kettle: a student prototype, not a medical device.*
