# Better than: how Kettle answers each top pain (Sat Nov 14, 2026)

Inputs: [`pain-points.md`](pain-points.md) (418 items, 153 with a pain, ranked by count × severity), the eight teardowns, [`feature-matrix.md`](feature-matrix.md) and [`ui-patterns.md`](ui-patterns.md). Every Kettle answer below is a *plan*; nothing is built yet. "Prove it" names the metric, test or demo moment that stage 3 must produce, and no number is claimed here.

Effort: S = under 2 h, M = 2–5 h, L = over 5 h, for a lead plus two beginners.

## Three things Kettle does that no incumbent does

1. **Publishes its accuracy, per activity, with N, against published systems.** No incumbent we found (havn, Assistiv, Howz, Canary Care, StackCare, Caregiver Aware, Care Daily, Alexa, Apple Sound Recognition) publishes a detection, miss or false-alarm rate. Kettle reports macro F1 and per-activity misses and false alarms on the public SINS week-long recording, on the unseen-microphone slice, against the DCASE 2018 baseline and winner, against **Apple's own built-in SoundAnalysis classifier** used zero-shot (303 classes, mapped to the 9 activities), and on our own kitchens as the honest worst slice.
2. **Shows its evidence under every bar.** Each timeline block carries the top sounds Kettle heard and how sure it is ("Dishes, pots and pans 0.36 · Cutlery 0.19"), and says "Not sure" instead of guessing. Incumbents show either raw sensor events ("Toilet opened 15:59", Howz) or bare summaries ("Bathroom 16 times, TYPICAL", StackCare) with no reason attached.
3. **Activity-level kitchen detail from one device the family already owns, with nothing to install and nothing leaving the house but labels.** Smart plugs know the kettle switched on; Kettle hears cooking vs dishes vs eating vs TV vs an empty room. Apple's on-device classifier stays with the phone's owner; havn and Assistiv need their own hardware and a cloud; Kettle needs a laptop or an old phone on a charger.

## What we deliberately won't build (and why)

| Not building | Why |
|---|---|
| Fall detection, emergency calls, EMS escalation | Safety-critical. We cannot validate it this weekend, and a missed fall is the worst pain in the data (theme 3). havn, Alexa Emergency Assist and pendants own this; Kettle says plainly "not an emergency device". |
| Smoke / CO / glass-break detection | Amazon's version fails 2 of 15 times and was withdrawn in the UK. A real smoke alarm is the right tool. |
| Any speech recognition, transcripts, voice calling or drop-in | Breaks the "no words kept" promise that answers theme 1. |
| A learned "unusual day" model | SINS clips have no clock times, so we could not measure it honestly. A caregiver-set rule is transparent and testable. |
| Accounts, cloud sync, a hosted share link, multi-home or agency dashboards | No-auth, no-database rule (AGENTS.md §4); each adds login bugs (theme 9) and a vendor that can disappear (theme 8). Siblings can open the same local page on the home network in the demo; real remote sharing is "with another week". |
| Medication tracking, vitals, Bluetooth devices | Off-track for the 60-second story and adds health-advice risk. |
| Native iOS/Android apps | A local web page runs on the laptop and phone browser with no store review. |
| Learning the parent's routine over weeks | Not needed: per-window labels work from minute one, and users don't complain about cold start, so there's no reason to buy that complexity. |
