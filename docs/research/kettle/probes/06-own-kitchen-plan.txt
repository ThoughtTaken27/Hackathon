- What: the same 9 SINS activities where they exist in a real kitchen. Target 8 activities x 5 sessions x ~1 min of

  predictions are committed. The clips are not shared outside the team.

  with N and a session-bootstrap CI. Expect a large drop vs SINS (different mic, room, person): it is the honest worst slice.
