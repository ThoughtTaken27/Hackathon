DECISION INPUT
- Every trained head lands ~76-82 on this tiny check; zero-shot (any model, any map) lands 40-49. Training a head is the
  step that matters, not the encoder.
- CED-tiny matches the best probe score at 1/40th of AST's CPU time (0.014 vs 0.52 s per 10-s window) and the least memory,
  so live mode on an old laptop and embedding 12k clips x 4 channels (~48k windows, ~9 min CPU) are both cheap.
- AST stays as the ablation encoder (and the "bigger model" comparison judges will ask about); CLAP stays as the zero-shot
  baseline. Re-decide on the full dev folds in stage 3 M3 (dev only, never the eval set).
