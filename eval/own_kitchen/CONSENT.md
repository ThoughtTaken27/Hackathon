# Consent form: kitchen recordings for the Kettle own-kitchen test

Kettle is a school hackathon project. This form is for one test: recording kitchen sounds so we can check how well Kettle works in a home it has not seen before.

## What is recorded

Sounds made in a kitchen while the test runs. This can include voices if you are nearby. We will not record conversations. For one session, two teammates may read a short scripted card aloud.

## Where it is kept

Only on the recorder's laptop, in a folder that is not shared. Recordings are never uploaded, never committed to the project's code repository, and never sent to anyone outside the team.

## What is published

Only two things:

- The labels: the start time, end time and sound type (for example "cooking") of each part of a recording.
- Kettle's predictions for those parts.

No audio is published. Ever, unless you tick "yes" in the last column below and sign a separate form later.

## Your rights

- You can say no. Saying no means you will not be recorded.
- You can withdraw at any time, before or after recording. Tell the recorder, and your recordings will be deleted from the recorder's laptop. Your row in the table will be marked "withdrawn".
- Initials only are written in this form. Do not write your full name.

## Signatures

Each person who can be heard in a recording writes one row. Fill in your initials, today's date, and answer both questions.
